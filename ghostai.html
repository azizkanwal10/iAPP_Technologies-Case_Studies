The brief

GhostAI is a real-time AI assistant for the Mac. It listens to the call, suggests answers as the conversation happens, takes notes, and turns every meeting into a summary with key points and action items.

In a live call there is no time to search for a fact, check a number or write things down. The goal was an assistant that keeps up with the conversation itself: one that listens, answers the moment a question comes up, stays out of sight when the screen is shared, and leaves a clean record of what was agreed.

Who it serves: Professionals who spend their day in calls: sales and client meetings, team stand-ups, interviews and presentations.

What made it hard

Answers in real time

Suggestions are only useful while the question is still being asked, so listening, understanding and answering had to happen in seconds.

Private on a shared screen

The assistant has to stay visible to its user but never appear when they share their screen in Zoom, Meet or Teams.

Works with every call app

People switch between meeting platforms all day, so GhostAI had to work across the major ones rather than inside a single app.

Nothing lost after the call

Notes, key points and action items had to be captured automatically and be there on every device afterwards.

What we built

Real-time suggestions

GhostAI listens to the conversation and suggests answers and talking points as questions come up.

Invisible when screen sharing

The assistant window stays private to you and does not show up when you share your screen.

Automatic notes and summaries

Key points, summaries and action items are captured for every call, with no manual note-taking.

Conversation memory

GhostAI saves memories of past conversations, so context carries over from one meeting to the next.

Works with your meetings

Plugs into Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls.

Native Mac app

Separate downloads for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, opened from the Ghost icon, with summaries that sync across every device you are logged in on.

How we worked

  1. Start from the live moment

    We designed around the seconds after a question is asked, keeping the path from hearing to answering as short as possible.

  2. Stay out of the way

    The interface is a small, private overlay that never competes with the call or shows up on a shared screen.

  3. Capture everything after

    Notes, summaries, action items and memories are produced automatically, so the meeting record writes itself.

  4. Ship for every Mac

    Native builds for Apple Silicon and Intel, with sync so summaries follow the user across devices.

Where it landed

A Mac assistant that answers in real time during Zoom, Meet and Teams calls, stays invisible on a shared screen, and turns every meeting into notes and action items.
