The brief

PDF Converter turns photos, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and web pages into PDFs and back again, with a full editor, e-signatures and an AI assistant built in.

People handle documents on their phones all day, but converting, merging or signing a PDF usually means a desktop. The app needed to put every common PDF task one tap away, work with the files people already keep in the cloud, and stay fast as the toolset grew.

Who it serves: Students, freelancers and office workers who need to get a document done without a laptop.

Inside the app

What made it hard

Dozens of tools, one simple home

Merge, split, compress, convert, sign, annotate and more had to stay discoverable without turning the home screen into a maze.

Conversions that just work

Word, PowerPoint, Excel, HTML and image formats each have quirks; output had to be reliable and quick.

Files live everywhere

Documents sit in Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, SharePoint, Files and Mail. The app had to meet them there.

What we built

Convert both ways

JPG, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and HTML to PDF, and PDF back to JPG, Word, PowerPoint and Excel.

Organise PDFs

Merge, split, compress, rotate, unlock and watermark.

Editor and annotation

Text, shapes, highlights, sticky notes and freehand drawing, plus a distraction-free reader mode.

Forms and signatures

Fill PDF forms and add, edit or remove digital signatures.

Cloud and security

Import from and upload to the major cloud drives, with password protection and encryption.

AI assistant

AI summaries, OCR and an assistant that finds the right tool, plus search inside file contents.

How we worked

  1. Lead with intent

    A smarter landing screen asks what you came to do and routes you straight to the right tool.

  2. Grow the toolkit

    New modules such as invoice and CV makers, a text editor, Excel editor and scroll-shot capture were added without crowding the core.

  3. Go global

    Localised into 25 languages and extended to iPad, Mac and Apple Vision.

Where it landed

Nearly five million downloads and a 4.4 rating across more than 6,000 App Store reviews.
