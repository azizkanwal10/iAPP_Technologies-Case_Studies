Screen Recorder

Record, react, edit and stream your screen from one app.

The brief

Screen Recorder captures games, tutorials, calls and live shows in HD, adds a face-cam reaction and voice commentary, and edits the result before it goes to YouTube, Facebook or Twitch.

Creators record on the phone but have to jump between apps to add commentary, trim and publish. The brief was one app that covers the whole loop: capture, react, edit and share.

Who it serves: Gamers, educators and creators making tutorials, demos and reaction videos.

What made it hard

Recording without lag

Capture has to stay smooth while a game or video is running, and start or stop from a floating control.

Reaction on top of content

Face-cam and voice commentary must line up with the recording and be adjustable clip by clip.

Editing on a small screen

Trimming, merging, music, text, speed and transitions needed to fit comfortably on a phone.

What we built

One-tap recording

Start and stop from the notification bar or a floating window, with external audio.

Live streaming

Stream your screen to YouTube, Facebook and Twitch.

Reaction camera

Face-cam overlay and real-time voice commentary on any video, web page or photo.

Video editor

Trim, merge, add music, text and subtitles, stickers, watermarks, filters, speed changes, crop and transitions.

Long screenshots

Stitch photos or generate long screenshots straight from a recording.

AI video module

Smart editing and automatic improvements to recordings.

How we worked

  1. Protect the capture

    Performance and stability came first, with recurring work on recording smoothness and crash fixes.

  2. Make reactions first-class

    The reaction camera and commentary were rebuilt as their own module.

  3. Localise and refresh

    A modern UI refresh and localisation into nine languages widened the audience.

Where it landed

More than three million downloads and a 4.6 average rating on the App Store.
