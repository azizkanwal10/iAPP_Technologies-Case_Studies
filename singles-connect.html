The brief

Singles Connect is a swipe-based dating and geosocial app. Members browse nearby profiles, swipe to like or pass, match, then chat with photo sharing and meet over in-app voice and video calls.

The client, Singles Connect 2022 Inc. of Hay Lakes, Alberta, is a relationship-focused company that also runs matchmaking, workshops and community events for singles. They needed an app that carries that approach onto phones: real people, safe conversations, and an easy path from a match to an actual conversation.

Who it serves: Singles in Alberta, across Canada and beyond who are looking for real relationships.

Inside the app

What made it hard

Trust from the first swipe

Fake profiles sink dating apps. Verification had to be strong enough to matter and simple enough that people actually complete it.

Real time, every time

Chat, photo sharing and voice and video calls all had to feel instant on both iOS and Android.

Close, but not too close

People want to know who is nearby without anyone seeing their exact location, so distance is shown as an approximation.

Paid plans without friction

Auto-renewing subscriptions had to work cleanly through both app stores, with clear terms.

What we built

Swipe and match

Swipe right to like, left to pass, or send a super-like. Mutual likes become a match.

Rich profiles

Photos, a short bio, occupation, height, interests and approximate distance.

Face verification

Members verify with their face, so matches know the profile is genuine.

Chat with photo sharing

Real-time messaging with pictures, opened straight from a match.

Voice and video calls

Meet face to face inside the app before meeting in person.

Subscriptions

Monthly, three-month and six-month auto-renewing plans through the App Store and Google Play.

How we worked

  1. Launch the core

    Version 1.0 shipped in January 2024 with swiping, matching and chat.

  2. Listen and adjust

    Releases replaced phone verification with email sign-in, removed swipe limits, added picture sharing in chat and a new profile design.

  3. Build trust

    Face verification and verified badges arrived to keep the community genuine.

  4. Polish and speed up

    Later versions brought in-app calling, smoother swiping and faster loading, through version 5.0 in September 2026.

Where it landed

Fifteen App Store releases since January 2024 and a 4.5 rating on Google Play, with swiping, verification, chat and video calls covered end to end.
